Before your paper goes anywhere.

Tunetrack Papers is a community space for musicians to share useful ideas, stories, reviews, systems, discoveries and experiences.

  • Write something you created or have the right to share. Give credit when someone else’s work or idea is involved.
  • Keep it respectful. No harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, targeted abuse, spam, or content intended to harm others.
  • If you attach an image, video, music or other media, make sure you have permission to use it. You are responsible for what you submit.
  • Tunetrack may review, edit for clarity or formatting, reject, or remove a submission when needed to keep the Papers space useful and safe.
  • Submitting a paper does not guarantee publication. We may contact you if something needs clarification.
  • By continuing, you confirm that the information you submit is yours to share and that you understand these guidelines.

That’s it. Make something thoughtful, useful, honest, or simply worth reading.
