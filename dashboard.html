HOW TO GUIDE ☻ 101.


SIMPLE GUIDES TO GET THE MOST OUT OF YOUR DASHBOARD.

TUTORIAL 01

1. UNDERSTANDING YOUR DASHBOARD

Your TuneTrack Dashboard is the central place for managing everything connected to your account. Once you sign in, the Dashboard gives you quick access to your products, freebies, account settings, downloads, and other available features.

Take a moment to familiarize yourself with the main navigation before moving on. Each section is designed to handle a different part of your TuneTrack account.

2. NAVIGATING THE DASHBOARD

The main navigation is located at the top of the Dashboard. From here, you can move between the different areas of your account without having to return to the main page.

Use My Account to manage your personal information and account settings.

Open My Products to view the products connected to your account.

The Freebies section gives you access to available free assets that you can claim and use.

3. FINDING YOUR PRODUCTS

Your purchased and claimed products are organized inside My Products. From there, you can review the products associated with your account and access the available product information or downloads.

4. MANAGING YOUR ACCOUNT

Your account settings can be accessed through My Account. Keep your information up to date so your TuneTrack account continues to work correctly.

5. USING THIS GUIDE

Each tutorial focuses on one specific part of your Dashboard. Open the guide you need, follow the explanation, and use the visual references on the left to understand where each feature is located.

QUICK TIP

If this is your first time using TuneTrack, we recommend going through the tutorials in order. This will give you a better understanding of how your account, products, freebies, and downloads work together.

TUTORIAL 02

1. GO TO FREEBIES

Open the Freebies section from your TuneTrack Dashboard. Here you can find the free products and assets currently available for you to claim.

2. CHOOSE YOUR FREEBIE

Browse the available freebies and select the one you want to download. Open the freebie to continue with the claiming process.

3. ENTER THE FREEBIE GATE

After selecting a freebie, you will be taken to the Freebie Gate. Follow the instructions shown on the page and click CONTINUE to proceed.

4. WATCH THE VIDEO

Watch the required video for approximately 1 minute. This short step helps support TuneTrack and allows you to continue with your freebie claim.

5. SUPPORT TUNETRACK

Before downloading, support TuneTrack by completing the requested actions such as following and subscribing to our channels.

6. DOWNLOAD YOUR FREEBIE

Once the claim process is completed, go to the My Products section in your Dashboard.

Your claimed freebie will appear there together with its available download information. Use the provided Google Drive link to download your files.

QUICK TIP

If your freebie does not appear immediately, refresh your Dashboard and check My Products again. Make sure you have completed every step in the Freebie Gate.

WATCH TUTORIAL ↗
TUTORIAL 03

1. GO TO OUR CATALOG

Open Our Catalog from the TuneTrack home screen. You can also access the catalog directly through the main Navigation Menu.

2. CHOOSE YOUR PRODUCT

Browse the available products and select the product you want to purchase. Open the product page to view its information and continue to checkout.

3. CHOOSE YOUR PAYMENT METHOD

At checkout, choose between Indonesia Payment or Global Payment.

For global purchases, you can pay using a supported card or PayPal.

4. COMPLETE YOUR PURCHASE

Follow the payment instructions shown on the checkout page and complete your payment. Once the payment has been successfully processed, your order will be ready for delivery.

5. RECEIVE YOUR PRODUCT FILE

Your purchased files will be delivered through Google Drive.

After completing your purchase, look for the green highlighted link on the checkout page. This link will take you to your product files.

6. CHECK YOUR PURCHASE IN YOUR EMAIL

You can also check the invoice sent to your email after purchase. The invoice contains your purchase information and can be used to access your delivery details.

NEED HELP?

If you experience an error with your payment, order, or product delivery, please report the issue through the Contact section on the TuneTrack website.

QUICK TIP

If you cannot find your product files immediately, first check the green highlighted link on the checkout page and the invoice sent to your email.
